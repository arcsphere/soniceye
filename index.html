How to use SonicEye

  1. Try the demo. A demo track is already loaded. Press ▶ or Space to preview it.
  2. Add your track. In Media, choose an audio file (mp3, wav, m4a), or drag it onto the preview.
  3. Add visuals. Optionally add a background (image or looping video), cover art and a logo. Drag-and-drop works: video goes to the background, an image to the cover art.
  4. Pick a look. In Template, choose a preset and an aspect ratio: 16:9 for YouTube, 9:16 for Shorts, 1:1 for square posts. Then tweak the visualizer, text and progress bar.
  5. Export. In Export MP4, render the first 10 s to check the look, then export the full track. The MP4 downloads when it's done.

Good to know

  • Your files never leave your computer. Everything is analyzed and rendered in your browser.
  • Your template settings are saved in this browser. Use Save template to keep a copy as a file.
  • Save .viz stores a track's analysis, so long tracks load faster next time.
  • MP4 export needs a recent Chrome or Edge.
